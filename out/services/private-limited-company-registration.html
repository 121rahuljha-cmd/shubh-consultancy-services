Overview

A Private Limited Company is the most widely chosen business structure in India for founders who plan to raise capital, onboard co-founders, or build a business that outlives its promoters. It is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and gives your business a separate legal identity from the people who own it.

Shubh Consultancy Services handles the complete incorporation on the MCA SPICe+ portal — from reserving your company name to receiving the Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, TAN and bank-ready documentation. You deal with one point of contact throughout.

Who needs this

  • Startups planning to raise seed or venture funding
  • Businesses with two or more co-founders sharing equity
  • Companies that need limited liability protection for personal assets
  • Founders who want to offer ESOPs to employees
  • Businesses bidding for corporate or government contracts

Documents required

  • PAN card and Aadhaar of all directors and shareholders
  • Passport-size photograph of each director
  • Address proof of directors (bank statement or utility bill, within 2 months)
  • Registered office proof — electricity bill and rent agreement
  • No Objection Certificate from the property owner
  • Passport (mandatory for foreign nationals or NRI directors)

What is included

  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for two directors
  • Director Identification Number (DIN) allotment
  • Company name reservation through RUN / SPICe+
  • Drafting of Memorandum and Articles of Association
  • Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies
  • Company PAN and TAN
  • Share certificates and first board resolution drafts

How the process works

  1. 1

    Requirement call & document collection

    We understand your shareholding plan, confirm director eligibility and share a single checklist of documents.

  2. 2

    DSC and DIN application

    Digital signatures are issued for each director and DIN is applied for through the MCA portal.

  3. 3

    Name reservation

    We run a trademark and MCA availability check, then file up to two preferred names for approval.

  4. 4

    SPICe+ filing with MOA & AOA

    Incorporation documents are drafted, e-signed and submitted along with the registered office proof.

  5. 5

    Incorporation certificate & handover

    You receive the Certificate of Incorporation, CIN, PAN, TAN and a post-incorporation compliance calendar.
