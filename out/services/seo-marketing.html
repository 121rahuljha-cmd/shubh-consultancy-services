Overview

Search is where most commercial intent begins. Someone looking for an FSSAI consultant in Ghaziabad types exactly that into Google — and clicks one of the first few results. SEO is the work of making sure that result is you.

We take a measurable approach: fix the technical issues holding the site back, build content around keywords that indicate buying intent, strengthen local presence through Google Business Profile, and report on rankings, traffic and enquiries every month.

Who needs this

  • Businesses invisible on Google for their own service terms
  • Local businesses competing for “near me” searches
  • E-commerce stores needing organic product traffic
  • Companies wanting to reduce dependence on paid ads
  • Websites that lost rankings after a redesign or algorithm update

Documents required

  • Website and hosting access
  • Google Analytics and Search Console access
  • Google Business Profile access, if claimed
  • List of your main competitors
  • Your priority services and target locations

What is included

  • Full technical SEO audit with a prioritised fix list
  • Keyword research mapped to commercial intent
  • On-page optimisation of titles, meta, headings and internal links
  • Google Business Profile optimisation and local citations
  • Content plan and blog articles
  • Monthly ranking, traffic and enquiry report

How the process works

  1. 1

    Audit and benchmark

    We record where you rank today, audit the site technically and analyse what competitors are ranking for.

  2. 2

    Strategy and keyword mapping

    Target keywords are chosen for commercial intent and mapped to specific pages, with gaps identified.

  3. 3

    On-page and technical fixes

    Speed, indexation, structure, metadata and internal linking issues are corrected.

  4. 4

    Content and authority building

    We publish content against the plan and build relevant citations and links each month.

  5. 5

    Measure and refine

    Monthly reporting on rankings, organic traffic and enquiries, with the next month's priorities agreed.
